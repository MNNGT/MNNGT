## 📊 Selected Projects

### 💰 Medico-Economic Evaluation of the 2023–2024 Dengue Epidemic in Martinique

Public Health Master's research project conducted during my internship at Santé publique France – Antilles.

- Evaluated the economic burden of a 47-week dengue epidemic, predominantly associated with DENV-2.
- Estimated total economic cost: **€12.81 million** (95% uncertainty interval: €10.82–15.17 million).
- Estimated cost per excess case: approximately **€729**.
- Combined multiple health data sources, including OpenDamir, OpenMedic and epidemiological surveillance data.
- Processed approximately **886 million raw records**, resulting in 2,277,069 rows in the consolidated analytical dataset.
- Conducted uncertainty analysis using **10,000 Monte Carlo simulations**.
- Developed reproducible R workflows for data processing and analysis.

**Tools:** R, data.table, ggplot2, fitdistrplus, Monte Carlo simulation.
